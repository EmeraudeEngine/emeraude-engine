# Point and spot lights lost their inverse-square falloff on 2026-08-12 — candela means nothing since

## Why

`src/Graphics/AGENTS.md` § *Light Attenuation — Physical, Not Artistic (since 2026-07-26)* documents a windowed
inverse square (Karis, SIGGRAPH 2013: `saturate(1 - (d/r)^4)^2 / (d^2 + 1)`), generated by
`Saphir/LightGenerator.PerFragment.cpp` (added `897c64b3e`, 2026-07-26). Commit `1c1d94ba` (2026-08-12, "the
Blinn-Phong machinery is gone — one lighting model, per fragment") DELETED that file. The only falloff generated
since is `max(1 - |d / r|², 0)` (`LightGenerator.PBR.cpp` ~446-454), with the same form in RTGI/RTR/the probes:
radius-bounded, no `1/d²`. Verified in code 2026-09-25 (found by the light-colour normalisation audit).

Consequences: a point/spot intensity in candela (or lumens through `setLuminousPower()`) is not a physical
quantity — a 2500 cd fire light delivers ~2500 × window at 1 m and at 10 m alike; the radius (documented as "a
culling bound, not a dimmer") IS the dimmer; every point/spot-lit demo tuned since 08-12 compensates for it; the
`Graphics::Photometry` comment (~157-159) that "the shader keeps applying the plain inverse-square law inside it"
and `SceneDataConsumer`'s radius derivation (`cullingRadiusFromIntensity`, ~303-311, ~340-346) rest on the lost law.

## Owner decision (2026-09-25)

**Restore the windowed inverse square and re-tune the demos**, as a lot of its own after the light-colour
normalisation is pushed: ONE shared GLSL helper for the raster light pass and the RTGI/RTR/probe direct terms (the
lanes must not drift), then every point/spot-lit demo re-tuned against it at a pinned exposure.

## What remains

1. Restore the windowed inverse square (raster light pass + the three RT direct terms, one shared GLSL helper so
   the lanes cannot drift), then re-tune every point/spot-lit demo (their numbers are "fixed" today against the
   wrong law).
2. Measure before/after on `light-and-shadow-debug`, `sprite`, `citadel`, `liminal`, `basic-scenery` at a pinned
   exposure; check the RT/raster parity (reflected/direct of one object in one frame).
3. Fix the stale texts: `Graphics/AGENTS.md` § Light Attenuation (flagged false 2026-09-25), `Photometry.hpp`
   ~157-159, `Scenes/Loaders/AGENTS.md` (~403), the "culling bound, not a dimmer" comments (`SceneDataConsumer.cpp`
   ~303-305 / ~330-339, `FX.cpp` ~85-86, projet-alpha `Marble.cpp` ~77-79).
